This Privacy Policy describes how the gm-console operations console and the underlying
Ada platform (collectively, the "Service") handle information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the Service:

- **Account information** — email, display name, organization name (when you create an account)
- **Operational data** — pipeline definitions, audit logs, runtime metrics generated by your
  workflows stored inside the platform you operate
- **Telemetry** — error traces and usage counters (opt-out available on self-hosted deployments)
- **Support correspondence** — anything you voluntarily send to our support channels

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

- Operate, secure, and improve the Service
- Provide customer support
- Send security and operational notices (where you have an account)
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. Legal Bases (GDPR / PIPL)

For users in the EU and China, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

- Performance of a contract (your use of the Service)
- Compliance with a legal obligation
- Legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention)
- Your consent (for marketing communications)

## 4. Data Storage and International Transfers

Data may be processed in the region you select at deployment time. Cross-region transfers
follow the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or PIPL standard contract requirements.

## 5. Sub-processors

A list of approved sub-processors is published at
`https://ada.kanvas.dev/legal/sub-processors` and updated at least 30 days before any change.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time;
we will delete within 30 days unless a legal hold applies.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR / PIPL / CCPA)

You have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion
- Object to processing or withdraw consent
- Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact: `privacy@kanvas.dev`

## 8. Security

Controls this repository can evidence:

- Role-based access control with a tenant middleware layer
- Audit logging (see `observability/`, and the per-run remediation audit trail)

Controls this repository does **not** evidence, previously asserted here
without qualification:

- ~~TLS 1.3 in transit~~ — no TLS is configured anywhere in `deploy/`.
  There is no Ingress and the Service is `ClusterIP`, so termination is
  the operator's choice and outside this repository. The in-cluster Redis
  link is documented as explicitly unauthenticated and unencrypted
  (`deploy/infra/ada-session-redis.yaml`).
- ~~AES-256 at rest~~ — no volume encryption is configured. This is an
  operator/CSI concern, not something this repository sets.
- ~~Audit logs retained for 365 days~~ — the shipped retention is **30
  days** (`observability/loki/loki-config.yaml`). 365 days appears only
  as an `Enterprise` plan entitlement in `crates/ada-billing/src/plan.rs`,
  and there is no paid tier to grant it.
- ~~SOC 2 Type II controls (in progress)~~ — there is no SOC 2 programme,
  no auditor engagement, and no managed offering to audit.
  `docs/commercial/PRESS_KIT.md` records the managed offering as roadmap,
  which makes this line and that one mutually exclusive.

The "RFC 8693" attribution on the access-control line was also wrong:
RFC 8693 is *OAuth 2.0 Token Exchange*. Token exchange is explicitly
unimplemented in this codebase (`crates/ada-identity/src/oidc.rs` returns
`token exchange not implemented`). The same citation appears in
`crates/ada-identity/src/session.rs` and should be reviewed separately.

## 9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

We will post any material changes to this Privacy Policy at least 30 days before they take
effect, and notify account holders by email.
